Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our affiliate program

How do I join the affiliate program?

Click the "Join the Program" button above and fill out the application form. Once approved, you'll get access to your affiliate dashboard with your unique referral link.

How much commission can I earn?

Commission rates start at 10% and can increase to 15% based on your performance. Check the Commission Tiers section for details on how to qualify for higher rates.

When and how do I get paid?

Payouts are processed monthly on the 15th for commissions earned in the previous month. Minimum payout is R50. Payments are made via bank transfer, PayPal, or Digi-Coins.

How are referrals tracked?

Referrals are tracked using cookies that last for 30 days. When someone clicks your link and makes a purchase within that period, you earn commission on their purchase.

What products can I promote?

You can promote any product on the DigiHive marketplace. Some products may have special commission rates - these will be marked in your dashboard.
